# Flick, vertical film web app

**Prepared by Greenlight** · A premium, filmmaker first home for vertical film.

This is the working scope for what we build first, what comes soon after, and
what we are deliberately leaving for later. The live Greenlight link gives the
founding team a way to weigh in on every item before we commit, so this document
is the starting position, not the final word.

---

## The wedge

Win with one thing done beautifully: a fast, full screen, swipe to next feed
that makes the filmmaker content effortless to watch in the browser — on mobile
web and desktop. Everything in the MVP exists to support that core loop. Depth
such as deeper discovery, creator tools, and richer monetization gets layered on
once people are hooked.

---

## Build now (MVP)

The smallest set that feels premium on day one and can actually launch and grow.

### Core player and feed
- Vertical full screen player
- Swipe to next feed
- Autoplay on scroll
- Tap to pause and double tap to like
- Resume where you left off

### Discovery and browse
- Curated or algorithmic home feed
- Search
- Filmmaker pages
- Continue watching rail

### Accounts and profiles
- Email and password sign up
- Apple and Google sign in
- User profile
- Watchlist or save for later

### Subscriptions and monetization
- Paywall or subscription gate
- Subscription tiers
- Free trial
- Stripe subscription checkout
- Manage and restore subscription (Stripe customer portal)

### Social and engagement
- Like or favorite
- Share to social or link
- Follow filmmakers

### Filmmaker tools
- Upload portal (basic submit and ingest)

### Admin tool and CMS (scaled down for launch)
- Content ingest and encoding pipeline
- Metadata management
- Content scheduling and publishing

### Playback quality and infrastructure
- Adaptive bitrate streaming
- CDN delivery
- DRM and content protection

---

## Soon (fast follow after launch)

Valuable, but they do not block the first release.

- Search filters, browse by category, collections and playlists, trending and editor picks
- Comments and ratings
- Web push notifications and new release alerts
- Creator dashboard and basic performance analytics
- Picture in picture, playback speed, manual quality selector, seek and scrubber bar
- Subtitles and captions
- Account settings, watch history depth, and magic link sign in

---

## Later (future, not right now)

Real features we want eventually. We are choosing not to build these for the
first release so we can ship faster and learn.

- **Email notifications**
- In app inbox
- Offline downloads
- Multiple audio tracks
- Localization and multiple languages
- Multiple profiles per account
- Promo codes and gifting
- Rent or buy individual titles
- Ad supported free tier
- Payout and revenue reporting for creators
- Submission and review workflow
- Moderation tools, user management, and a full reporting dashboard
- Watermarking and advanced piracy protection
- Casting to AirPlay and Chromecast

---

## Key decisions for the team

These two calls drive the whole timeline. The Greenlight link asks each person
to weigh in.

1. Which experience do we optimize for at launch? Desktop web first, mobile web first, or both from day one.
2. Admin or content tool for launch? Full tool, scaled down, or manual for now.
